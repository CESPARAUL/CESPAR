Research with Us

We are committed to building regional and institutional database for advancing research in atmospheric and space science and associated technological innovations – towards significant contribution to sustainable development in Nigeria, Africa and the world over.

We welcome viable collaborations that can create impact leading to mutual benefit. Please contact us for further interactions if you would like to collaborate or develop partnership with us.

We have the following resources for research purposes:

VLF Receivers

Very Low Frequency receivers for terrestial atmospheric data collections.

Weather Station

Our weather station captures rainfall, temperature, humidity, wind speed. wind direction. sun intensity etc

Network

We are connected to many international space stations and share their sattelite information. We collate data from India, Germany, United Kingdom, USA etc

Research Team

We host researchers all over the globe. There has been rigorous research works that has led to publication of many papers in international journals. Join us.

About CESPAR

Center for Space Research (CESPAR), Anchor University Lagos aims at addressing the dearth of research data in the region through deployment of multiple ground-based facilities, and strengthening institutional capabilities in atmospheric, solar-terrestrial and radio science research. Among other research areas, we exploit data from very low frequency (VLF) Radiowaves receiver and other ground-based facilities (e.g., weather station) to study atmospheric and ionospheric irregularities including investigation of the diurnal and seasonal changes, atmospheric-lithospheric connections, solar-induced perturbations and their space weather implications.

CESPAR was established to strengthen AUL capabilities in space sciences and research through coordinated and collaborative research and mentorship. We pursue vigorous research in atmospheric and radio sciences, solar-terrestrial Physics and space weather, to address the challenges posed by space weather to both ground-based and space-borne technology. Thus contributing to sustainable development in Nigeria, Africa and globally.

The mandate of Anchor University is straightforward: Raising young men and women who are academically sound and spiritually vibrant for national and global transformation. Moreover, with the divine approval under which we operate, our success is sure.

Anchor University is a unique University born in due time to address the gaps in quality education in Nigeria and beyond. But more importantly focused on raising exceptional leaders that bear the mark of Christ and are poised to brighten their corners across different walks of life, globally.

The vision is to be a citadel of learning for holistic human transformation and development anchored on the tripartite wheels of Character, Competence and Courage. This vision has found expression in our Outcome-Based Education approach, which is IT-driven by diligent and competent staff in a most serene environment. We are determined to create a model University that is poised to compete with the best in the world.

Established not to add up the numbers but to make a clear difference. In this journey to making a positive difference, we have with us the unflinching support of our Saviour, the steadfast Anchor in life’s stormy sea; unmistakable guidance through God’s immortal Word, the Bible; and the undaunted commitment of our proprietor-base, the Chancellor, Pastor (Dr) W.F. Kumuyi and the entire members of the Deeper Christian Life Ministry worldwide.
